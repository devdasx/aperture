Your Privacy Choices

Aperture is built so there is almost nothing to choose out of. The app has no account system, no advertising identifiers, no analytics, no tracking cookies, and no Aperture server that receives your wallet data.

1. Personal data we can act on

Aperture does not collect personal data through the iOS wallet. We cannot view, export, correct, sell, share, or delete wallet data from our systems because your wallet data is generated and stored locally on your device.

No sale or sharing: Aperture does not sell personal information, share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, or build advertising profiles.

2. Choices on your device

  • Delete local wallet data: delete the Aperture app from your device. The wallet data stored in the app sandbox is removed by iOS.
  • Remove an optional iCloud backup: if you created an encrypted backup, manage or delete it from your Apple iCloud storage settings.
  • Limit network exposure: use a VPN or iCloud Private Relay if you want blockchain RPC and explorer providers to see less network-level information.
  • Control permissions: camera, Face ID, and photo permissions are managed in iOS Settings.

3. Blockchain data cannot be deleted by Aperture

Public blockchain transactions are recorded by the network itself. Aperture cannot remove, hide, reverse, or edit transactions after they are broadcast. This is a property of public blockchains, not a data collection practice by Aperture.

4. Privacy requests

If you contact us by email, GitHub, or another support channel, we may receive the information you choose to send in that message. You can ask us to delete or respond to that support communication. Do not include your recovery phrase, private keys, PIN, or backup password in any message.
